# Privacy Policy for LyricSnap — Lyric Card Maker for YouTube Music

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

**LyricSnap — Lyric Card Maker for YouTube Music** does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or listening activity. All lyric card creation and preferences stay strictly on your device.

## What Data We Store Locally

1. **Saved Card Preferences (Local Storage):** To remember your visual card preferences across sessions, the extension saves your selected card theme preset (for example, Blurred Art or Midnight) and card size (`1:1`, `9:16`, or `16:9`) locally inside your browser on your own device.
2. **Temporary Social Share Image (Session Storage):** When you click "Share on X" or "Share Facebook", the extension temporarily holds your generated lyric card PNG image in browser session memory for up to 90 seconds solely to attach it into the newly opened X or Facebook post composer, after which it is immediately cleared.

## How Data Is Used and Processed

- **Lyric Card Creation:** When you open the LyricSnap floating window on YouTube Music, the extension reads the currently playing song's title, artist, album cover image, lyrics text, and song link on your screen solely to render your downloadable lyric card image inside your browser.
- **No Off-Device Transmission:** None of this information is ever sent to any developer server or analytics service.
- **Social Sharing:** When you click "Share on X" or "Share Facebook", your browser opens the official X (`x.com`) or Facebook (`facebook.com`) composer in a new window and attaches the lyric card image and song link you chose to share.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use cookies, trackers, analytics, telemetry, or third-party servers.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved card design preferences remain stored locally on your device until you change them or uninstall the extension. Temporary social share images are removed immediately after being attached (or when you close your browser). Uninstalling the extension immediately deletes all locally stored preferences.

## Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices ever change, we will update this policy and bump the "Last updated" date above prior to releasing the updated extension version.
